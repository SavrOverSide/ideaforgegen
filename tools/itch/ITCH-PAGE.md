   - **Short description or tagline:** 100 system-agnostic seeds where the steppe meets dead satellites.
   - **Classification:** Physical game  (так itch называет книги и материалы для настолок)

     (Если хочешь сначала собрать отзывы: «No payment / donate», а через пару недель поставить цену.)

5. Нажми Save, потом поменяй Visibility на **Public**.

## Description (по-английски, вставить в поле Description)

**Yurts next to server racks. Dombras made of wolf bone. Oracles with dead smartphones.**

*Steppe & Static* is a 14-page system-agnostic book of 100 ready-to-use seeds for game masters, writers and game designers. Every entry is built to drop straight into your game, whether it is D&D, Mörk Borg, Cyberpunk RED, Blades in the Dark or something you made up last night.

**Inside:**
- **20 Characters**, each with a habit and a secret
- **20 Artifacts**, each with a power, a price and a legend
- **20 Quests** with a client, a reward and a twist
- **20 Locations** with a mood, a secret and a danger
- **10 Factions** that want something badly
- **10 Game Pitches** for video-game and board-game designers
- **10 Campaign Kits**: a place, who rules it, their secret, a job and a prize, already linked into one evening of play

**How to use it:** roll a d20 or d10 on any table, mix parts between entries, or just read a page before your session and steal what sticks.

**What you get:** a printable A4 PDF with clean two-column layout, easy to read on a tablet or print at home.

You may use anything in this book in your own games and in published work, commercial or not. Please do not resell the PDF itself.
